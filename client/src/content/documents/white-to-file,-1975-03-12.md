This is a memo to file, written by Pepperdine executive vice president [[Howard A. White]], dated March 12, 1975. In the memo, White expresses dissatisfaction with the religious direction of the school under president [[William S. Banowsky]].

>March 12, 1975
>
>Yesterday [[M. Norvel Young|Norvel]] stopped by my office a few minutes after we had had a meeting with [[William S. Banowsky|Bill]] and others about a proposed school of Public Affairs Administration. I seized the opportunity to tell Norvel that I thought he ought to make an issue with the trustees the matter of [[Royce Clark]]’s view of Jesus. I told him I thought the attitudes are fast polarizing, that most people now sense the difference in philosophy between the Chancellor and the President, that the Chancellor (Norvel) still has the power to sway the Trustees if he wishes, and that even if he were to lose, with the trustees, I think it would be better to take a stand and risk a loss of vote than to allow Bill to keep cutting the dog’s tail off an inch at a time.
>
>He said he did not want to to \[*sic*\] that, for he just might not win, and also he thiinks \[*sic*\] that Bill may get dissatisfied wihh \[*sic*\] his job at Pepperdine and decided \[*sic*\] to take something else. In my opinion this is just wishful thinking. Where is Bill going to get a job that will pay him $57,000 a year, furnish a house that is a palace overlooking the ocean, other expenses and perquisites, plus all the time he wants to get into politics and earn another large sum of money. (He bragged last year that he made over $100,000).
>
>I reminded Norvel that although it is not his nature to get tough with dissidents, that he had shown he could do it with a few faculty with, in my opinion, lesser offenses than some that are now occurring. But he still thinks this is not the time to make an issue of things. [[Helen Mattox Young|Helen]], bless her heart, was up this week-end among the ladies of [[Associated Women for Pepperdine|AWP]] trying to convince them that the cause here is not lost. Bill boasted yesterday that while Helen was meeting with the AWP, [[Gay Banowsky|Gay]] (his wife) was skiing with the women of the [[Fritz Huntsinger]] family at Mammoth, and that was far better than being with the AWP. Norvel zaid \[*sic*\] that Bill has talked with [[Blanche Seaver|Mrs. Seaver]] enough about the church that she no longer goes to [[Inglewood Church of Christ|Inglewood]].
>
>\[handwritten:\] [[Bill Stivers]] told me yesterday what [[James Smythe]] had said some time ago: Pepperdine is much more liberal now than when Norvel came—
>
>It is reported that Bill favors setting us a theological school--run of course by the three liberals whose faith is often questioned., \[*sic*\] or by their kind. There is no market for the product. I told Norvel Bill has already put us in the role of a religious prostitute, and now he wishes to hand \[*sic; recte:* hang\] the red light over the door.
