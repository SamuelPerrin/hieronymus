This is a letter from Pepperdine president [[William S. Banowsky]] to [[Doyle Swain]], the director for special projects at Pepperdine’s Center for International Business, dated June 1, 1973. In the letter, Banowsky assures Swain that, despite rumors to the contrary, he has no plans to run for the U.S. Senate in 1974.

>June 1, 1973
>
>Dear [[Doyle Swain|Doyle]]:
>
>I enjoyed our recent conversations and wanted to reinforce to you, in writing, my very firm commitment to continue serving at Pepperdine for as long as I can foresee in the future. I am aware of some recent rumors that I plan to challenge [[Alan Cranston|Senator Cranston]] next year. I do not know the source of these rumors, but I enclose one or two newsletters which I think have fanned the flame. None of the authors of these reports have been in contact with me in any way whatsoever. In addition, I understand that [[George Putnam]] mentioned something about the possibility on his television broadcast. I haven’t talked with George, but I understand that his announcement was based on the newsletter published in the San Fernando Valley.
>
>Apparently none of the leading Republicans want to challenge Senator Cranston and, as a result, many people are looking about for some new, qualified candidate. Despite the rumors, I can state categorically that I will not be a candidate against Senator Cranston.
>
>You are deeply acquainted, Doyle, with our thrilling work at Pepperdine. I have had the rare experience of working with the Malibu campus from its very inception. I love it almost as a parent loves a child. As you know, the new [[Brock House|President’s home]] is being completed. Gay has worked two years on these plans, and we are excited about finally moving to the beautiful new campus itself. As you also know, we face some financial challenges as a result of completing the first phase of construction, and I feel the deepest sense of personal responsibility in meeting the University’s obligations.
>
>Thank you for your friendship.
>
>Sincerely,
>\[unsigned\]
>William S. Banowsky
